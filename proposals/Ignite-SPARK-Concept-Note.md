## Project
PBAC CellScope: a low-cost, AI-powered label-free live-cell imaging prototype for diagnostics and drug screening.

## Problem
Advanced live-cell imaging systems cost Rs 20–40 million each and are imported; Pakistani hospitals and labs cannot afford them, so cell-level analysis stays manual and slow.

## Solution
An open-hardware quantitative phase / holotomographic imager combined with an open AI stack (Cellpose, micro-SAM, Ultrack) that segments, tracks and scores living cells without stains, producing automated reports on viability, morphology and drug response.

## Functional prototype (18 months)
1. Months 1–6: AI pipeline validated on public CC0 data (already in progress, open on GitHub).
2. Months 4–12: imager build; benchmark vs a commercial reference.
3. Months 10–18: pilot at a partner lab — viability and drug-response assays on cell lines; Tibb formulation screening track.

## Verifiable results
Resolution and accuracy benchmarks; pilot deployment report; open code; patent filing (PCT) for device design elements where novel.
