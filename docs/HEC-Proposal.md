## Title
AI-Driven Label-Free 3D Cell Intelligence: An Open, Low-Cost Platform for Live-Cell Tracking and Drug-Response Prediction in Pakistan

## Problem
Cell-level analysis in Pakistani labs relies on stained, fixed samples and manual microscopy. Live 3D imaging and AI analysis exist globally but are expensive and imported. Local researchers cannot follow living cells over time or quantify drug response at scale.

## Objectives
1. Build and validate an open AI pipeline (segmentation → tracking → features → forecasting) for 3D time-lapse microscopy, benchmarked on the CC0 Biohub dataset.
2. Generate the first Pakistani label-free live-cell dataset at the partner lab.
3. Demonstrate a label-free drug-response assay, including screening of standardised traditional (Tibb) formulations.
4. Design a low-cost open-hardware imaging unit and benchmark it.

## Methodology
- Imaging: holotomography/QPI (label-free) and light-sheet (developmental/organoid samples).
- AI: Cellpose, micro-SAM, Ultrack, Trackastra; gradient-boosting and temporal deep models for fate/response prediction.
- Validation: tracking metrics vs ground truth; viability assays as reference; specialist review.
- Ethics: IRB approval for any human-derived material; de-identified data.

## Deliverables (24 months)
Open-source pipeline; local dataset; 2 journal papers; validated assay; hardware prototype design; trained students.

## Relevance to Pakistan
Lowers the cost of advanced cell biology; builds indigenous AI-biomedical capacity; links traditional medicine to measurable evidence.

## Indicative budget heads
Compute and storage; lab consumables and microscope time; open-hardware components; RA/student stipends; publication.
