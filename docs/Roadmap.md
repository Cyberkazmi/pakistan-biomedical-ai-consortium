# PBAC Roadmap

Version 0.2 · Draft

| Phase | Window | Goal | Deliverables | Indicative cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0. Software proof | Nov 2026 – Apr 2027 | Prove PBAC can build working cell-intelligence AI | Reproduced Biohub tracking result; open `pipeline/` (segment → track → features → forecast); public demo notebook; short technical report | Near zero (Kaggle/Colab GPUs) |
| 1. Local data | May – Oct 2027 | First Pakistani dataset | Partner-lab access agreement; ethics (IRB) approval; label-free / fluorescence time-lapse of local samples; first assay (cell viability / drug response) | Low (lab access, consumables) |
| 2. PBAC device | Nov 2027 – Oct 2028 | Low-cost open-hardware imager | Open QPI or light-sheet build (~$20k–50k); benchmark vs commercial system | SPARK / NRPU scale |
| 3. Validation | 2028 – 2030 | Clinical relevance | Prospective study with hospital partner; DRAP pathway; Tibb formulation validation track with NCT | Grants |
| 4. Moonshot | 2030+ | In-vivo cellular-scale imaging research | International consortium | Large-scale |

## Immediate actions (Phase 0)
1. Run `pipeline/` on Biohub CC0 data; reproduce a public top solution.
2. Publish results and a demo in this repository.
3. Secure a faculty Principal Investigator at a partner university (required for HEC NRPU).
4. Register PBAC (or a lead startup/SME) as a legal entity, required for Ignite SPARK and most grants.
5. Submit concept notes as calls open (Ignite SPARK, HEC NRPU).

## Funding calls being tracked (Oct 2026)
- **Ignite PM's SPARK**: up to Rs100M per project, functional prototypes, 18 months; concept deadline 26 Oct 2026, full proposals end Nov 2026. Lead can be a university, research org, startup or SME.
- **HEC NRPU 2026–27**: outline deadline 30 Oct 2026, up to 24 months; PI must be university faculty (PhD/FCPS).
